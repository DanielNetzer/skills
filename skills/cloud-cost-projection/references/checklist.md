# Checklist

## Before modeling
- [ ] Scope agreed: entities in/out, allocation method, cash basis, KPI and its revenue basis.
- [ ] Baseline invoice read line by line; project × product matrix reconciles to the sub-total to the cent.
- [ ] Cost-module baseline within ~4% of the invoice by project (explain the gap: IT items, attribution, timing).
- [ ] Every contract has minimum, term, cadence, uplift, renewal date, and evidence it was executed (a posting on the invoice).
- [ ] CUD terms and end dates captured; initiative start months gated on them.
- [ ] Credit program: earned, remaining, thresholds, progress; crossing month estimated per scenario.
- [ ] Terminating commitments (unused savings plans) have an end month or a placeholder flagged as such.

## After building (all four must hold)
- [ ] recalc: 0 formula errors.
- [ ] Reconciliation difference ≈ 0.
- [ ] Every Shares row = 100%.
- [ ] Breakdown grand total = Projection net; Cash by project total = vendor cash total (every month, within cents).
- [ ] Baseline month totals match the actual bill (usage + support + postings).

## Deliverable framing (what CFOs asked for)
1. Plan-year net in the report currency and % of revenue per scenario; gap to the cap; state whether the revenue basis matches the cost scope.
2. Committed share of spend (what cannot move until renewal) and the renewal calendar.
3. Peak cash month and its cause (usually one annual vendor fee plus a quarterly installment); whether a billing-mode switch flattens it.
4. The three assumptions that move the answer most, with the input cell for each.
5. Excluded-but-paid: what the company pays for entities outside the projection, and the recommendation to bill them separately.
6. Gaps register with owners and dates: unread order forms, unsigned mods, unknown end months, credit schedule, revenue basis.
