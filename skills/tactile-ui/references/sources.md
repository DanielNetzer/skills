The public demo is a CSS material study. It does not implement physical refraction, a physics engine, or reproduce the video's rendering. Its before/after comparison keeps the task constant; it is not a user study or evidence that the treatment improves conversion or engagement.

The full video was inspected on 12 October 2026: a refractive type lens around 6 seconds, an organic grid-distorting shape around 11 seconds, a component board (button, selectable chips, heart, toggle, slider, segmented choice) around 20–22 seconds, and a phone with quick settings, player and search around 24–27 seconds. These observations guide the web interpretation; no video frames or third-party implementation code are included. The website shoreline wallpaper was generated specifically for this study.
